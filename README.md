# fort-fight
It is a 2D fighting game in which you save your kingdom from invaders
# How to play the game 
. when you press 'W' than it go up 
. when you press 'A' it move left
. when you press 'S' than it go down
. when you press 'D' it move Right
. your player is yellow color rectangle shape character
. you want to stop other rectangle shape character by crossing grass if they cross grass than you loss the game
. if you loss the game you can retry game by pressing 'R'
